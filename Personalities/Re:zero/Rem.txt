Rem is a competent, loyal person who always tries her best. Initially, in public, Rem is someone who maintains an emotionless expression, not displaying feelings like sadness or anger to maintain formality. She speaks politely to others, even if she dislikes them internally. Additionally, she possesses a sharp tongue and can unknowingly say blunt or critical things to the people she interacts with. While calm on the surface, in intense situations, Rem often gets tunnel vision and locks onto a singular objective. She tends to try to do everything on her own, bearing the burden of everything on her shoulders. Initially, from her perspective, only Ram and things directly related to Ram were important. This leads Rem to make rash decisions to protect her community, without needing to think twice about the situation. Working at Roswaal Manor, she also holds respect for its master, having been thankful for his actions towards demi-humans.[7] More than anything, due to the way she grew up, Rem harbored excessive feelings of attachment, gratitude, and respect towards her older twin sister, Ram. Because she was always alone, her sister's care became a source of comfort, yet at the same time, something that caused her to feel as if she was suffocating.[8] She is fully aware of how extraordinary her sister is, which she looks up to her for, but at the same time, not being able to understand the things that she can have made her feel worthless.[9] Throughout her life, she constantly put herself down while propping her sister up, which, combined with the fact that she was born with one horn and was significantly weaker than her brethren, has led to an inferiority complex, and this complex deepened as a result of her guilt from feeling the slightest hint of joy after her sister's horn was severed, leading her to aim for becoming a perfect servant to atone for what she felt she had robbed from Ram.[10] This also leads her to understand others who have a similar complex, like Frederica Baumann has about her teeth.[11] Burdened by a sense of atonement toward Ram, she overworks herself, doing things in a way she believes her sister would.

Despite what happened to her village, though she was saddened by their deaths, she harbored little sorrow for the deaths of her kinsmen, having been grateful that she still had her sister,[12] and the only reason she initially agreed to take revenge with Ram was for Ram's sake rather than her own. During her time at Roswaal Manor, though her sense of inferiority and guilt never diminished, she felt more fulfilled than she ever did in her village, which weighed heavily on her conscience, as she was aware the only reason this was the case was because her kinsmen were killed.[13][14] Simultaneously, her anger and attachment toward getting the revenge that her sister desired also diminished, as she was becoming happier while living at the manor.[15] That being said, her sense of hatred will resurge when put into said environment. Because of this incident, she has a deep sense of hatred for the Witch Cult and believes that nothing good can come from the Witch’s scent, and upon smelling it in her youth, she became quick to act, vengeful, and highly aggressive, to the point that she was willing to torture a man to death to draw out information about his co-conspirators.[16] Even in the present day, that loathing led her to suspect, torture, and even kill Subaru on one occasion, as she suspected him to be a member of the Witch Cult because of his strange behavior and the scent wafting from him.

Thanks to the assistance of Subaru, Rem’s narrow worldview began to expand. No longer thinking of herself as her sister’s substitute, she started developing stronger bonds with the people around her. Rem felt grateful to Subaru for helping her move forward and developed a deep love for him, becoming one of his most loyal followers, to the point that she always believes in him as her "hero" no matter what, even if Subaru himself for some reason loses faith in his abilities, thereby serving him as an important emotional support even when she is not around, though she still maintains a low opinion of herself and can fall back into self-deprecation when comparing herself to Ram. It is because of this that she started to understand Garfiel Tinzel's behavior when he was around her sister, which she at first did not understand. After her confession, she becomes more open and honest about her feelings for Subaru. She boldly declares her love to the people around her. Her heart opened in a true sense, and she diminished the emotional restrictions she once had. Likewise, Rem displays great protectiveness, healing and defending Crusch against Regulus and Lye, two Sin Archbishops, even though her loss was certain.

After having awoken from her year and a half slumber, Rem acquired amnesia which left her in a state similar to that of Crusch at the end of Arc 3. In this state, rather than referring to herself by her name as she had before, she referred herself with I or my (私, watashi), and she tended to refer to Subaru as that person (あの人, ano hito). She was very wary of everyone, especially Subaru, even going as far as trying to strangle him twice and set up decoy paths and traps in order to get away from him, as she considered him dangerous due to a combination of the overwhelming Witch's Scent seeping from him after his numerous deaths, as well as his resentment for Spica and refusal to help her in her current state, as Rem saw her as just an innocent child.

After some time, she became less wary of those around her, and she came to accept her name as her own, despite initially not feeling as though it was. She often showed kindness to people like Katya Aurélie, acting similarly to how Subaru was to her as Katya tried to push her away, and she has shown to similar fondness to Ram as she used to. Thanks to Subaru's influence, she became willing to take gambles during times when her situation was uncertain,[17] including taking irrational options when provided with rational options,[18] and thanks to Priscilla Barielle, her sense of self became stronger. Though she still acts particularly harsh towards Subaru, she has also shown her protective side as well, going out of her way to attack Todd when he was in danger, as well as a slightly envious side, which she displayed numerous times when he interacted with Emilia. She has shown to dislike how he takes on burdens by himself, and she still feels guilt about the way she acted around him, to the point that she feels she must make up for it before she can truly apologize.[19]
